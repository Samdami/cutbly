Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Cutbly. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

General Questions

Cutbly is a modern URL shortening service that helps you create short, memorable links from long URLs. We also provide powerful analytics, custom branding, and link management tools to help you track and optimize your online presence.

Yes! Cutbly offers a free plan that includes basic link shortening, analytics, and link management features. We also offer premium plans with advanced features like custom domains, team collaboration, and priority support for power users and businesses.

You can create shortened links without an account, but signing up unlocks powerful features like link management, detailed analytics, custom aliases, and the ability to edit or delete your links. It's free to create an account!

Security & Privacy

We use military-grade encryption (TLS 1.3) to protect all data in transit and at rest. Our servers undergo regular security audits and compliance checks. We never sell or share your data with third parties, and all your information is stored securely in encrypted databases.

Yes! We automatically scan all URLs for malware, phishing attempts, and malicious content. If a suspicious link is detected, we block it immediately and notify the link creator. We also provide link previews so users can see where a link leads before clicking.

We collect anonymous analytics data including: click timestamps, referrer information, geographic location (country/city), device type, browser, and operating system. We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) and all data is anonymized in compliance with GDPR and privacy regulations.

Link Management

Yes! You have complete control over all your links. You can delete any shortened link from your dashboard at any time. Once deleted, the short URL will no longer redirect to the original destination and will show a "link not found" message.

No, shortened links don't expire automatically. They'll remain active indefinitely unless you manually delete them. This means you can create permanent short URLs that work forever. Premium users can optionally set custom expiration dates for time-sensitive campaigns.

Yes! If you're logged in, you can edit the destination URL of any shortened link you've created. The short URL stays the same, but it will redirect to the new destination. This is useful for updating campaign links without changing the short URL you've already shared.

Absolutely! You can create custom aliases for your links (e.g., cutbly.com/myevent) instead of random characters. Custom aliases must be unique, between 3-20 characters, and can contain letters, numbers, and hyphens. This makes your links more memorable and branded.

Analytics

Our analytics are highly accurate, tracking 99.9% of clicks. We use advanced detection algorithms to filter out bot traffic and provide you with genuine click data you can trust. All data is updated in real-time, so you can monitor your link performance as it happens.

You can view total clicks, click trends over time, geographic data (countries and cities), referrer sources, device types (mobile/desktop/tablet), browsers, operating systems, and peak click times. Premium users get access to more detailed reports and data exports.

Yes! Premium users can export analytics data in CSV or JSON format for further analysis. This includes all click data, timestamps, geographic information, and device details. You can also integrate with analytics platforms via our API.

Technical Questions

Yes! We offer a comprehensive REST API that allows you to integrate Cutbly into your applications. You can create, manage, and track shortened links programmatically. API access is available for free accounts with rate limits, and premium users get higher limits and priority support.

Yes! Premium users can connect their own custom domains (e.g., go.yourbrand.com) for branded short links. This helps build trust and brand recognition. Setup is simple—we provide step-by-step DNS configuration instructions, and our support team is ready to help.

Free accounts can create up to 100 links per month with unlimited redirects. Premium accounts have higher limits: Pro users get 1,000 links/month, Business users get 10,000 links/month, and Enterprise users have no limits. All plans include unlimited link clicks and redirects.
